# Honorable Mentions: V. Humbled by the World

_5 additional books that belong in this category_

---

**At the Tomb of the Inflatable Pig**, Matthew Parris (South America / Paraguay) — Wandering through Paraguay, South America's forgotten country, uncovering bizarre dictators, utopian colonies, and a nation time forgot.

**In a Sunburned Country**, Bill Bryson (Oceania / Australia) — Bill Bryson's hilarious journey through Australia, a continent trying to kill you with sharks, spiders, jellyfish, and exceptional friendliness.

**Notes from a Small Island**, Bill Bryson (Europe / UK) — Bryson's affectionate farewell to Britain after 20 years, celebrating eccentricity, warm beer, and a nation of apologetic queue-lovers before moving home.

**The Innocents Abroad**, Mark Twain (Europe / Holy Land) — Mark Twain's satirical steamship tour of Europe and the Holy Land, skewering American tourists and Old World pretensions with sharp wit.

**The Lost Continent**, Bill Bryson (North America) — Bill Bryson searches for the perfect American small town, finding mall sprawl, nostalgia, and the gap between Norman Rockwell dreams and reality.
