# Honorable Mentions: IX. Self-Reckoning Through Movement

_7 additional books that belong in this category_

---

**Between the Woods and the Water**, Patrick Leigh Fermor (Europe) — Patrick Leigh Fermor's elegant sequel continuing his walk across 1930s Europe, from Hungary through Romania as storm clouds gather.

**Stranger to History**, Aatish Taseer (Multiple / Muslim World) — An Indian-Pakistani son searches for his estranged Muslim father, journeying through the Islamic world seeking identity, faith, and belonging.

**The Art of Travel**, Alain de Botton (Multiple) — [Description needed for The Art of Travel]

**The Colossus of Maroussi**, Henry Miller (Europe / Greece) — Henry Miller's ecstatic ode to Greece, celebrating light, friendship, and ancient landscapes that liberate the soul from modern despair.

**The Geography of Bliss**, Eric Weiner (Multiple) — A grumpy journalist travels to the world's happiest and unhappiest countries, seeking the formula for national contentment from Iceland to Moldova.

**The Songlines**, Bruce Chatwin (Oceania / Australia) — Bruce Chatwin explores Aboriginal songlines—the invisible paths sung into existence that map Australia's landscape through myth and dreamtime.

**Wind, Sand and Stars**, Antoine de Saint-Exupéry (Africa / Sahara) — Antoine de Saint-Exupéry's lyrical meditation on flight, the Sahara, and humanity, from the author who would later write The Little Prince.
