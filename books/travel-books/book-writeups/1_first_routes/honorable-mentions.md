# Honorable Mentions: I. First Routes and Foundational Journeys

_5 additional books that belong in this category_

---

**A Lady's Life in the Rocky Mountains**, Isabella Bird (North America / Colorado) — Victorian lady Isabella Bird rides solo through Colorado's Wild West in 1873, defying corsets and convention to climb mountains and befriend outlaws.

**Around the World on a Bicycle**, Thomas Stevens (Multiple) — The first person to circle the globe on two wheels, crossing continents in the 1880s before paved roads existed.

**The Journal of a Tour to the Hebrides**, James Boswell (Europe / Scotland) — Samuel Johnson and James Boswell's 18th-century ramble through the Scottish islands, capturing Highland life before the modern world arrived.

**The Journals of Captain Cook**, James Cook (Pacific) — Cook's meticulous logs from three Pacific voyages, charting unknown waters and encountering indigenous peoples from Tahiti to Antarctica.
