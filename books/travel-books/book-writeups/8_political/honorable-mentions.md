# Honorable Mentions: VIII. Political Moral and Historical Reckonings

_6 additional books that belong in this category_

---

**A Dragon Apparent**, Norman Lewis (Asia / SE Asia) — Norman Lewis travels through French Indochina in 1950, just before the wars, capturing Vietnam, Cambodia, and Laos on the eve of destruction.

**A Turn in the South**, V.S. Naipaul (North America) — V.S. Naipaul's journey through the American South, exploring race, religion, and identity in the land of faulkner and civil rights struggles.

**Balkan Ghosts**, Robert D. Kaplan (Europe / Balkans) — Traveling through Yugoslavia as it fragments into war, tracing centuries of ethnic tension and history repeating itself in the Balkans.

**Imperium**, Ryszard Kapuściński (Europe / Soviet Union) — Kapuściński travels through the crumbling Soviet Union, witnessing the death of an empire and the human costs of totalitarianism's collapse.

**The Zanzibar Chest**, Aidan Hartley (Africa) — A war correspondent's family memoir intertwined with African conflicts, uncovering his father's colonial past through a mysterious wooden chest of secrets.

**Travels with Herodotus**, Ryszard Kapuściński (Multiple) — Kapuściński carries Herodotus as his guide while reporting from revolutionary Iran, India, and China, finding ancient patterns in modern conflicts.
